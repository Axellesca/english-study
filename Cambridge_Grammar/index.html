English Grammar: Understanding the Basics

Evelyn P. Altenberg (Hofstra University) y Robert M. Vago (Queens College / CUNY). Cambridge University Press.

Guía paso a paso no técnica: partes del discurso, frases y oraciones, con Test yourself, Getting started, Quick tips y claves de respuesta.

  • Part I — Kinds of words: Unidades 1–8 (Nouns, Verbs, Determiners, Adjectives, Prepositions, Conjunctions, Pronouns, Adverbs)
  • Part II — Kinds of phrases: Unidades 9–14 (Noun, Prepositional, Verb, Auxiliary phrases, Subjects and objects, Compound phrases)
  • Part III — Getting started with sentences: Unidades 15–17 (Functions, Combining, Related sentences)
